# Focus brief — Diana Ojeda

Written from Adrian's direction, 2026-08-06. Read this before writing or
editing any Diana Ojeda copy: the card, the case study body, or anything on
dianaojeda.ca itself.

This brief covers **what to say about this project**. Shape, length and
structure are the same for every case study and live in
`docs/case-study-guide.md`. Read both.

## What the project is about

Diana Ojeda is a Gestalt coach in Toronto. Her practice already had a home on
awarenessandflow.com, a site she co-founded, but that site was not built
around what she offers. It did not carry her vision, and it did not represent
her practice visually.

**The engagement, in one sentence:** a personal site under her own name that
feels as warm as her practice, explains what Gestalt coaching is and what it
rests on, and leads a visitor from the feeling they arrive with to the reason
her work could help them.

Three things that sentence is carrying, all of which have to survive into the
copy:

- **Hers, under her name.** Not a section of a shared site.
- **Warmth, and an explanation.** The site has to feel like her *and* make an
  unfamiliar practice legible.
- **A path, not a menu.** The visitor starts from how they feel and is walked
  forward. Be careful what you claim the first step achieves: naming the
  feeling and reading it described back gets them ready for how Diana would
  address it. It does not yet tell them why her work would help. That comes
  after they follow the link.

## Lead with

1. **The path through the site.** A visitor identifies the feeling they came
   in with, and the site opens out from there into how she works. Describe
   what the visitor does and what happens next. Never call it progressive
   disclosure or anything else from the design vocabulary.
2. **The lotus.** The graphic that represents her practice and the foundations
   it rests on. This is how the site explains where Gestalt comes from and
   what sits underneath it.
3. **The design.** Warm and colourful, deliberately not sterile or clinical.
   She wanted something alive. Worth noting that we got there together,
   through a lot of references, rather than handing her a palette.

## Do not say

- **"The room."** Recurring across projects and always wrong. There is no room
  in this one at all. See also `.planning/focus/02-aga-beauty.md`.
- **Anything built on "hold."** "What you need to hold", "the emotion you are
  holding", "a site that holds the three together". Wellness-copy filler.
- **"Progressive disclosure"**, or any other design-process term. It is what
  the site does; it is not what the copy says.
- **The internal design argument.** The current draft narrates our own
  reasoning at length: "The obvious build is three service pages, and that is
  the problem rather than the answer", "The reason to build it that way is
  that the interaction does the work rather than describing it", and an entire
  closing paragraph on the constraint that governs future edits. None of that
  is for a reader.
- **Implementation mechanics.** Radio groups, keyboard operability,
  JavaScript-off degradation, what a crawler sees. Real, and not in a case
  study.

## Fixed language

- **Clients**, not patients.
- **Gestalt coaching**, and the practice named plainly.
- **dianaojeda.ca**, her own site, distinct from awarenessandflow.com.

## Facts in the current draft — UNVERIFIED

These come from the existing case study and have not been confirmed by
Adrian. Check before any of them goes into new copy.

- Three modalities: Gestalt, Zen Shiatsu, Biodanza.
- Twenty years of embodied practice.
- Accreditations from the International Coaching Federation and the Gestalt
  Institute of Cleveland.
- Four states in the check-in: Overwhelmed, Stuck, Disconnected, Unfulfilled.
- The call to action is a free exploration call.
- Palette of ocean, clay and sage on warm cream.
- Four pages: Home, About, Work With Me, Contact.
- Every state routes to the same Work With Me page rather than to its own.

## Open question

The current subtitle, `cardDescription` and `featureImage` caption all hang
entirely on the check-in interaction. Adrian's direction reframes it as the
**first step of a path**, not the whole story. The copy should follow the
reframe, which means the subtitle and card need rewriting too, not just the
body.
