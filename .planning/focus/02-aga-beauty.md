# Focus brief — AGA Beauty

Written from Adrian's direction, 2026-08-06. Read this before writing or
editing any AGA Beauty copy: the card, the case study body, or anything on
agabeauty.ca itself.

This brief covers **what to say about this project**. It says nothing about
shape, length or structure, which are the same for every case study and live in
`docs/case-study-guide.md`. Read both.

## What the project is about

Malu Alburqueque is a certified esthetician running a private studio in
Burnaby. The work was already premium; nothing online showed it.

**The engagement, in one sentence:** designing a site that looks premium and
presents her, her services and her studio as an attractive, personalized
choice, while highlighting the trust signals she and her work have earned.

That sentence governs. Two halves, and copy that carries only one of them has
missed: the **personalized** half (her, her services, her studio, presented so
a stranger wants to choose *her*) and the **earned** half (the trust signals
are hers, not a design flourish we added).

## Lead with

How the sentence above breaks down. Not a menu to pick one from:

1. **The premium look.** How the site was designed to match the standard of
   the work.
2. **Trust signals, above all the five-star Google reviews.** Every review on
   her listing is five stars. That record is the credibility engine of the
   whole site.
3. **The services, presented simply and easy to follow.** A first-time client
   can read the full menu without working for it.
4. **The testimonials**, given real space.
5. **The studio itself.** Her own photographs of the room,
   edited by us so they look as inviting as the space is.
6. **Malu's immigration background.** A deliberate strategic call, not a
   biographical detail: a large share of her clientele are Latin American
   immigrants who recognise their own entrepreneurship and dedication in hers.

## Do not say

Each of these has been rejected. Reasons included so they don't get
re-derived from scratch.

- **"One client at a time."** Salon and esthetics work is one client at a
  time by definition. It distinguishes nothing and has been refuted
  repeatedly. Say **one-on-one care** or **one-on-one attention** instead.
- **"One client at a time is what a busy salon cannot sell."** Factually
  wrong for the same reason. Busy salons sell exactly that.
- **"No storefront, no team, no retail floor."** Carries no information a
  reader needs and reads as a list of things she lacks.
- **"No website at all."** Not important. She is early in the business, and
  plenty of businesses her age have no site. It also reads badly.
- **"Without pretending she is bigger than she is" / "we did not dress the
  studio up as something bigger."** Nobody accused her of pretending
  anything. Answering an accusation nobody made.
- **"We made the smallness the premium thing."** This was the old strategic
  thesis of the case study. It is not the story. The story is that the
  presentation was raised to meet work that was already premium.
- **Real prices instead of an enquiry form.** True, and worth a line inside
  `solution`, but it is a normal thing to do, not a strategic achievement.
  Never the hook.
- **"We built her a brand" / "we created her identity."** We did not. The
  engagement was the website. Say we **designed the site**, and describe the
  premium look as a property of the site, not of an identity we made her.

## Fixed language

- **one-on-one care** / **one-on-one attention**, never "one client at a time"
- **private studio in Burnaby**, not "no storefront"
- **Malu Alburqueque**, certified esthetician

## Safe facts

Verified and usable. Anything not on this list needs checking before it goes
into copy.

- Every review on her Google listing is five stars; the score is fetched live
  from Google at build time, so it cannot go stale.
- Nails, body and skin, in a private studio in Burnaby.
- Four pages: Home, About, Services, Contact.
- Booking runs through Fresha in a new tab, with the new-client fee waived
  and UTM parameters stripped so bookings aren't miscredited to Instagram.
- Built mobile first; on a phone a sticky bar runs along the bottom of every
  page with Book Now, WhatsApp and her number on it.
- Her Instagram grid is fetched at build time rather than rebuilt by hand.
- Her experience is a year and a half, in her own words. Her introduction runs
  close to verbatim.
- Launched end of July 2026 at agabeauty.ca. First draft approved with no
  notes beyond a few service prices.
- She has her own login: she can see traffic and request copy or price
  changes without emailing anyone.

## Claims that do not exist

Not confirmed, so they stay out: how the room smells or sounds, any team,
any second location, press, credentials she does not hold, invented reviews.
